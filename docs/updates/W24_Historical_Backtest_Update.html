Why survival alone is not enough

WHAT THE OUTCOME DATA ACTUALLY SAYS  ·  SURVIVAL AT APR 4, 2026
Active 210 · 87.5% Inactive 16 · 6.7% Acquired 2 · 0.8% Uncertain 8 · 3.3% Unknown 4 · 1.7%

A system that simply answered Active for every company would score about 87.5% and tell us nothing. A fund does not get paid for picking companies that merely still exist.

This is not a yes-or-no classification problem. It is a ranking and portfolio-selection problem: out of 240, which would you have backed, and in what order?

The metric is not settled, and the model comparison has not been run. There are no results to show yet.
